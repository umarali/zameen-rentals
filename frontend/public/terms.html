Finding a home

ZameenRentals is a rental aggregator. Listings currently come from Zameen.com, which we're independent of. Listing content belongs to its owners.

Confirm rent, availability and details with the advertiser before visiting or paying. Listings can change and map pins may be approximate. View the home and check who you're dealing with before paying a deposit. Rental agreements are between you and the landlord or agent.

AI suggestions and automated labels can be incomplete or wrong. Check details that matter to you with the advertiser.

Credits and payments

  • Search and the free features stay available without buying credits. The pricing page ↗ lists paid actions and their costs.
  • Buy credits once. There's no subscription or automatic top-up. Prices are in Pakistani rupees, and checkout shows the total before you pay.
  • Credits don't expire. They pay for actions on ZameenRentals and aren't transferable to other people.
  • Credits are linked to your browser. Keep your restore code private and save it before clearing browser data. It moves purchased credits to another device; free starter credits stay on the original device.
  • One credit pays for one Tailored list, one Search report or one daily shortlist for one saved search. If a list or report fails, its credit returns automatically. Empty lists, days without new shortlist matches and reports with too little activity use no credit.

Payment problems and refunds

Request a refund within seven days of payment for any unused purchased credits, including the unused part of a pack. We calculate it at the price per credit you paid. Free starter credits have no cash value.

If a payment is missing credits or you want to request a refund, open Search rentals and select Send feedback. Include your email and payment ID, or the payment date and amount. Don't send card details or your restore code.

Nothing in these terms takes away rights you have under applicable consumer law.

Fair use

Don't bypass credit charges or rate limits, disrupt the service, bulk-scrape listings, misuse restore codes, or use advertiser details for spam. We may restrict access that breaks these rules.
