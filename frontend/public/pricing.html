Use all your credits on one feature, or mix them however you like. Daily shortlists use credits while switched on. Stop them at any time.

If a Tailored list or Search report fails, its credit returns automatically. Empty Tailored lists and reports with too little activity don't use a credit.

When credits run out

Credit features stop when your balance runs out. Top-ups are not open yet, so you cannot add more credits for now. Search and the free features keep working.

When payments open, you will be able to buy credits in My rentals, then Top up. Checkout will show the available payment methods before you pay.

Prices are in Pakistani rupees. You can request a refund for unused purchased credits within seven days of payment, including the unused part of a pack. Read the refund terms and privacy policy.
