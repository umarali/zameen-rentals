Privacy policy

You can search without an account. This policy explains what we collect, why we use it and how to contact us.

Last updated 14 October 2026

Your browser and saved activity

A random ID in your browser links you to saved homes and notes, hidden homes, recent views, alerts, notification subscriptions and credits on our server. We also record first and last visits. No name, phone number or email is needed to search.

Your browser remembers preferences and keeps local copies of saved activity, recent results and feedback waiting to send. Our app doesn't set cookies. To limit repeated free-credit claims, we store a keyed fingerprint of your IP address and the date. For IPv6, we group addresses from the same network. Fingerprints and their daily keys older than two days are cleared on the first new claim of each day. Claim outcomes stay with credit records. PostHog analytics and session recording are off.

Searches and extra features

We record search filters and result counts without your browser ID or IP address in the search-history record. Popular and recent searches are visible to other visitors.

We send typed searches to Anthropic to turn them into filters. AI features also send your brief, relevant listing details, search criteria or figures calculated from your saved activity. We don't attach your browser ID. Avoid putting personal details in searches or briefs.

Anthropic doesn't train on API data by default. Its standard retention period is up to 30 days, with exceptions for safety and legal requirements.

Near me sends coordinates with your search and remembers them in your browser for 30 minutes. Request logs can contain those coordinates. Where voice search is available, our server transcribes the audio without storing it; the resulting text works like a typed search.

Payments, feedback and notifications

We keep credit transactions and payment records, including the payment ID, pack, amount and status. Checkout handles payment details; we don't store your card details.

Feedback includes your message, optional email and search context, such as search text, filters and map position. We store it and send a copy through Resend so we can respond. Notifications use a stored push subscription and your browser's push service.

Storage and retention

We host the service on DigitalOcean in Frankfurt, Germany. We retain a recovery copy of the database on the hosting server. Automatic backups are currently unavailable. Services named here may process data outside Pakistan.

Alert matches are kept for 30 days. Parsed searches are reused for seven days using a fingerprint of the text and its filters. Paid AI results are reused for 15 minutes; expired results are cleared when new results are saved.

Saved activity, subscriptions, feedback, search history, credit and payment records have no automatic expiry. Server logs record IP addresses, request times and URLs, which can include search text and location. Logs have no fixed retention period.

Other services

Photos from Zameen.com, maps from OpenStreetMap and Esri, and Google Fonts receive your IP address when your browser loads them. WhatsApp handles messages you choose to send to advertisers. We don't sell your data or share it for advertising.

Your choices and contact

Remove saved homes and alerts, unhide homes in My rentals, or turn off notifications. Clearing browser storage removes your local ID and preferences, but doesn't delete server records. Save your restore code first if you want to move purchased credits to another browser.

For privacy questions, data requests or concerns about an advertiser's details, open Search rentals and select Send feedback. Include an email for a reply. Don't send your restore code.

We'll update this page and its date when our practices change. See the terms of use.
