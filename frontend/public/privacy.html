Privacy policy

You don't need to give us your name, phone number or email to search. This page lists what we do collect, why, and how long we keep it.

Last updated 11 October 2026

Who runs ZameenRentals

ZameenRentals is run by [OPERATOR NAME], an individual sole proprietor in Pakistan. It isn't a registered company. Contact: [CONTACT EMAIL].

Your random ID

On your first visit, your browser creates a random ID and keeps it in local storage. The app sends it with requests for saved homes, alerts and credits, so our server knows which ones are yours. The ID isn't linked to your name.

If you clear your browser data, your browser creates a new ID and you lose access to anything linked to the old one. A restore code can move your credits.

What we store on our server

  • Saved homes (up to 500) and any note you add to them.
  • Homes you hide (up to 500) and the last 100 homes you opened.
  • Search alerts (up to 25), their filters, and the new matches we find for them. We delete matches after 30 days.
  • When you first and last visited, so we can show what's new since your last visit.
  • Your credits: each free grant, purchase, spend, refund and restore, with the time.
  • Payments: the payment ID from our payment provider, the pack, the amount, whether the payment went through, and the time.
  • Notification subscriptions, if you turn notifications on.

You can delete saved homes, hidden homes and alerts yourself in My rentals. Everything else stays until you ask us to delete it. We keep payment records for as long as tax law requires.

Searches

When you search with filters, we record the city, area, property type, bedrooms, price range, furnished choice, sort order and number of results. We don't record your ID or IP address with it. We use these records to show popular and recent searches to everyone.

When you type a search in your own words, we send the text and the city to Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, which turns it into filters. If Claude isn't available, our own parser does this on our server. We keep a one-way fingerprint of the text with the filters it produced and reuse it for 7 days, so the same search is faster next time.

Anthropic says it deletes these requests within 30 days and doesn't use them to train its models by default. Please don't type personal details such as a phone number or CNIC into the search box.

Paid AI features

A tailored list sends what you describe and details of the candidate listings to Claude. A search report sends your saved search filters and figures we computed from our listings data. A daily shortlist sends the saved search filters and that day's matching listings. We don't send your ID, name or contact details with any of them. We keep a result on our server for up to 15 minutes so a retry doesn't charge you twice, and we count daily AI use per feature without linking it to you.

Near me and voice search

Near me asks your browser for your location. If you allow it, the app sends the coordinates with that search. We don't save them in our database, but they appear in our server logs. Your browser keeps them for 30 minutes so you aren't asked again straight away.

Where voice search is offered, it records up to 15 seconds after you tap the microphone. Our own server turns the recording into text with an open-source speech model. We don't save the audio or send it to anyone else. The text then works like a typed search.

Notifications

If you turn on notifications, your browser gives us a push address and keys, and we save them with your browser's name and version. Your browser's push service, run by Google, Mozilla, Apple or Microsoft depending on your browser, delivers our messages. We delete the subscription when you turn notifications off or when the push service tells us it no longer works.

Feedback

Send feedback sends us your message, your email if you give one, and details of your current search: the search box text, filters, map position and number of results. We store it and email a copy to ourselves through Resend, an email service. We use your email only to reply to you.

Payments

When you buy credits, you pay on our payment provider's checkout. The provider handles your card or wallet details. We never see or store them. The provider tells us the payment ID, the amount and whether the payment went through.

Analytics

The site contains code for PostHog analytics, but it is switched off. If we switch it on, we'll update this section first. It would record pages viewed, clicks, which filters you use and which listings you open or contact, and replays of some visits with typed and on-screen text hidden. It would set a cookie and local storage entries to recognise return visits.

Server logs and abuse protection

Our server logs each request: the IP address, the time, the address requested and the result. The address can include your filters, the text of a typed search or Near me coordinates. We use logs to fix problems and stop abuse, and keep them for [LOG RETENTION PERIOD].

To limit abuse, the server counts requests from each IP address in memory for a few minutes. These counts aren't saved.

Stored on your device

We don't set cookies. The app keeps these in your browser's storage:

  • Your random ID.
  • A copy of which homes you saved or hid, so the app shows them before the server answers.
  • Your last city and filters, your compare list and your map style.
  • Whether you've seen the welcome tips and tour, and whether you dismissed the install prompt.
  • Feedback waiting to send while you're offline.
  • Your Near me location, for 30 minutes.
  • An offline copy of the app and of recent results, so it opens without a connection.

You can clear all of this in your browser settings.

Services your browser contacts

Some content loads straight from other services, which see your IP address as any website would: listing photos from Zameen.com, map tiles from OpenStreetMap, satellite images from Esri and fonts from Google Fonts. Calling or messaging an advertiser opens your phone or WhatsApp.

Where data is kept

Our server and daily database backups are with DigitalOcean in Frankfurt, Germany. We keep backups for 180 days. Anthropic, Resend and our payment provider process data on their own systems, some of them outside Pakistan.

Who we share data with

Only the services named on this page, for the jobs described. We don't sell your data or share it for advertising. We may disclose data if Pakistani law requires it.

Advertisers

Listings show the advertiser's name and phone numbers as published on Zameen.com. If you're an advertiser and want your details removed from ZameenRentals, contact us.

Children

ZameenRentals is for adults looking for a home to rent. It isn't meant for children, and you must be 18 or older to buy credits.

Your choices and requests

You can delete saved homes, hidden homes and alerts in My rentals, turn off notifications, and clear your browser data at any time.

To get a copy of the data linked to your ID, or to have it deleted, contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL]. We may ask for something that shows the data is yours, such as a restore code or payment ID. We'll reply within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top. For important changes, we'll also post a notice in the app before they take effect.
