A few useful answers

Where do listings come from?

Currently Zameen.com. Graana and OLX are planned; Facebook is exploratory. Those additional sources are not connected yet.

Are the homes still available?

We cannot confirm availability. Contact the advertiser to check the rent, details and availability before visiting. Map pins may show an approximate area.

Do I need an account?

No. Saved homes, alerts and credits are linked to this browser, not to an account. Clearing browser data or switching devices can lose access. A restore code moves your credits to another device.

Is rental search free?

Yes. Search, filters, maps, contacts, saved homes, comparisons and search alerts are free. A few extra features use credits.

What are credits?

Credits pay for extra features, such as a tailored shortlist or a daily shortlist. New visitors get 10 free credits. After that, you can buy a pack. The pricing page lists each pack and what each feature costs.

How do I pay?

You pay once at checkout with JazzCash, Easypaisa or a debit or credit card. Our payment provider handles the payment. We never see or store your card or wallet details.

Will I be charged again?

Never. There's no subscription and nothing renews. You pay for a pack once, and we don't keep your card.

Do credits expire?

No. Credits stay in your balance until you use them.

Can I use my credits on another device?

Yes. In My rentals, get a restore code on the device that has your credits, then enter it on the other device to move them there. Keep the code private, because anyone with it can move your credits. If you clear your browser data without a restore code, we may not be able to recover your credits.

Can I get a refund?

If a paid feature fails, your credits go back automatically. If you paid but didn't get your credits, use Send feedback in the app, leave your email, and we'll add the credits or refund you. You can get a refund for a pack within 7 days of buying it if you haven't used any of its credits. The terms of use have the details.

What happens to my data?

We don't ask for your name, phone number or email to search. Saved homes, alerts and credits are stored against a random ID that your browser creates. Searches you type in your own words go to Anthropic's Claude to be turned into filters. The privacy policy lists what we collect and how long we keep it.
