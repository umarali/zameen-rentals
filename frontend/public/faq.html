Search rentals

What is ZameenRentals?

ZameenRentals is an independent rental search tool for Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad. You can browse a map, filter listings, compare homes and save searches.

Where do the listings come from?

Listings currently come from Zameen.com. We plan to add Graana and OLX and are exploring rentals posted on Facebook. Those sources are not connected yet.

Is ZameenRentals free?

Yes. The current beta costs PKR 0 to use. Search, saved homes, comparisons and saved-search alerts are included. No payment card is needed. Property rent, deposits and any agent fees are separate.

How do I search in English or Roman Urdu?

Choose a city, then type an area, property type, bedroom count and budget. Try "2 bed flat DHA under 50k" or "DHA mein 2 bed flat 50k tak". Check the interpreted filters and adjust them if needed.

Are all listings still available?

We do not confirm availability for every listing. A property may already be rented or its details may have changed. Confirm the rent, availability and viewing arrangements with the advertiser.

What do listing dates mean?

A source date is a posting or update date provided by Zameen.com. First seen records when our system encountered a listing. Recorded date may use either type, so it is not an availability check. A newer date does not prove the home was newly listed.

Are map pins exact addresses?

Some listings include a location pin; others use an area location. Treat map locations and distances as guidance and confirm the address before visiting.

How do saved homes and alerts work?

Use the heart button to save a home. Use Get alerts to save your current search, then open My rentals to see new matches. Device notifications are optional. Your saved state is tied to this browser; clearing browser data or switching devices may lose access to it.

Is ZameenRentals part of Zameen.com?

No. ZameenRentals is an independent product and is not affiliated with Zameen.com, Graana, OLX or Meta.

How can I report an issue or suggest a feature?

Open the rental search and select Feedback. Include the listing link or describe what happened. You can use the same form to suggest a feature.
