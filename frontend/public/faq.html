A few useful answers

Where do listings come from?

ZameenRentals aggregates rental listings, currently from Zameen.com. Each home links to its original listing.

Are the homes still available?

We cannot confirm availability. Contact the advertiser to check the rent, details and availability before visiting. Map pins may show an approximate area.

Do I need an account?

No. Saved homes, alerts and credits are linked to this browser, not to an account. Clearing browser data or switching devices can lose access. A restore code moves purchased credits to another device.

Is rental search free?

Yes. Search, filters, maps, contacts, saved homes, comparisons and search alerts are free. A few extra features use credits.

What are credits?

One credit pays for one Tailored list, one Search report or one day of Daily shortlist for one saved search with new matches. New visitors get 10 free credits. You can buy 50 more for Rs 500 and spend them on any mix of these features. See the pricing page.

How do I pay?

You buy a pack once. Checkout shows the available payment methods and amount before you pay. We don't store your card details.

Will I be charged again?

There's no subscription or automatic top-up. You choose when to buy another pack.

Do credits expire?

No. Credits stay in your balance until you use them.

Can I use my credits on another device?

Yes, for credits you bought. After your first purchase, the payment confirmation shows a restore code. In My rentals, choose Restore code to create a replacement if you missed it. Enter it on the other device to move your bought credits there. Free starter credits stay on the first device. Keep the code private, because anyone with it can move your credits. You can replace the code from the device that has your credits. If you clear your browser data without a restore code, we may not be able to recover your credits.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Request a refund within seven days of payment for unused purchased credits, including the unused part of a pack, at the price per credit you paid. Use Send feedback in the app and include your email and payment ID, or the payment date and amount. Failed Tailored lists and Search reports return their credit automatically. See the terms and conditions.

What happens to my data?

We don't ask for your name, phone number or email to search. Saved homes, alerts and credits are stored against a random ID that your browser creates. Searches you type in your own words go to Anthropic to be turned into filters. The privacy policy lists what we collect and how long we keep it.
