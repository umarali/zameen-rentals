A few useful answers

Where do listings come from?

Currently Zameen.com. Graana and OLX are planned; Facebook is exploratory. Those additional sources are not connected yet.

Are the homes still available?

We cannot confirm availability. Contact the advertiser to check the rent, details and availability before visiting. Map pins may show an approximate area.

Do I need an account?

No account is needed for the current app. Saved homes and alerts are linked to this browser. Clearing browser data or switching devices can lose access.

Is rental search free?

Yes. Search, filters, maps, contacts, saved homes, comparisons and standard search alerts are free. An optional paid plan is being explored; it is not available yet.
